## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the
  overall community.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any
  kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Technical disagreement

This section is specific to this project rather than inherited, because
Knap's working method invites a kind of argument that is easy to mistake for
rudeness.

**Disagreeing with a claim is not an attack on the person who made it.** This
repository asks for evidence rather than reasoning: a gate is not passed
until its condition has been run and observed, and a benchmark number without
its machine, its input, and its spread is not a number. Asking someone to
produce that evidence, or saying plainly that a claim does not survive it, is
the project working as intended.

The same applies in the other direction. The author has published two
performance claims in this repository that turned out to be measurements of
the wrong input, and both are recorded as corrections rather than quietly
removed. A contributor who catches something like that is doing the project a
service, and should say so directly.

What is not acceptable is attaching the disagreement to the person: their
competence, their experience, their background, or their motives. Say the
claim is wrong and why. Do not say the person is careless.

## Scope

This code applies within all project spaces, and also when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces. Representation includes
using an official email address, posting from an official account, or acting
as an appointed representative at an event.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the author,
at the email address in [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md).

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The
privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

This is a single author project. That means a report goes to the same person
who maintains the code, and if the complaint concerns the author's own
conduct, there is no independent party inside the project to escalate to.
Saying so plainly is better than implying an impartiality that does not
exist. In that case, GitHub's own reporting mechanisms are available and
appropriate.

## Consequences

| Level | Behaviour | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Correction | Language or behaviour deemed unprofessional or unwelcome. | A private, written warning explaining the nature of the violation. A public apology may be requested. |
| Warning | A violation through a single incident or series of actions. | A warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with those involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing this code, for a specified period. |
| Temporary ban | A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behaviour. | A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the project for a specified period. |
| Permanent ban | A pattern of violation, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals. | A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project. |
